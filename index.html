Saturday 17 October 2026 6PM – 10PM 📍 International Youth Center

THE EVENT

On Saturday 17 October, Asian Society and Trakia University filled the International Youth Center in Stara Zagora with colour, culture and good company. Students from all kinds of backgrounds came dressed in their cultural clothes to play games, eat together, get henna done and celebrate side by side.

HOW THE NIGHT WENT

  1. 6PM: We started on timeDoors opened and everyone arrived in their best outfits.
  2. Opening & BingoA warm welcome from our hosts, then bingo to break the ice.
  3. FoodChicken tandoori, chana chaat and chai for everyone.
  4. TriviaTeam trivia with music, with every team on its own buzzer.
  5. Desserts & Chai, Musical ChairsSweet treats, then a very competitive round of musical chairs.
  6. Final scores & Best Dressed awardsScores, votes and a few proud winners.
  7. 10PM: That's a wrap!A night to remember.
